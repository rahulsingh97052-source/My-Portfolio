The Journey

My curiosity in technology began when I realized that software is not just an arbitrary set of instructions, but an active canvas where ideas take shape. As an IT undergraduate, I delve into computer architecture, data structures, network protocols, and frontend software engineering.

Parallel to code, I developed a passion for visual communication, typography, and UI/UX design. I believe software shouldn't just run efficiently—it should feel responsive, intuitive, and visually harmonious.

Today, I operate at this intersection. Whether architecting a web app, refining typography in design systems, or experimenting with generative AI pipelines, my goal is always to deliver experiences that are as technically sound as they are aesthetically considered.
